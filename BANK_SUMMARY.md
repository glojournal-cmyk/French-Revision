## Prompt rule

Every card begins with a visible label such as **TRANSLATE INTO FRENCH**, **TRANSLATE INTO ENGLISH**, **CONJUGATE**, **COMPLETE THE FRENCH**, **CHANGE THE TENSE**, **CORRECT THE ERROR**, **READ AND ANSWER**, or **CHOOSE THE ANSWER**. The instruction and required answer format are stored in `task` and repeated inside `prompt`.
